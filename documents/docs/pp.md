# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** 12/11/26

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains **what data we collect, how it is used, and how it is handled** when you use or purchase from any of our services (each website, platform, or storefront we operate — collectively "our services").

By using or purchasing from our services, you confirm that you have **read, understood, and accepted** this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree, **do not use or purchase**.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect **only the minimum data required** to provide our services.

### Account information
- **Discord User ID and/or Username** — if you sign in with Discord. Used for identification, support, and recordkeeping.
- **Name, email address, and profile image** — if you sign in with Google or GitHub. Provided by the provider at sign-in and used to identify your account.

### Device information
- **Device UDID** — required to issue your iDevice certificate to the correct device.
- Basic device details (device model, device type, iOS version) — used to process your order correctly.

### Purchase information
- Order records: what you purchased, the price, the payment method used, and the order status.
- Basic transaction references from our payment providers (for example: transaction ID, amount, payment status).

We do **not** collect:
- Credit card numbers or bank details
- Billing addresses
- Names or addresses (except where you provide them via a sign-in provider)
- Tracking data, or any unnecessary personal information

---

## 2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used **only** to:
- Process and deliver your certificate
- Verify purchases and keep order records
- Provide customer support
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and payment disputes
- Keep internal records of your orders

We do **not** use your data for:
- Advertising
- Tracking
- Analytics
- Marketing
- Selling or sharing with third parties for their benefit

---

## 3. Payment Providers & Other Third Parties

Payments are processed by third-party payment providers, including:
- **Stripe** (card payments)
- **Razorpay** (UPI / Indian payment methods)
- Crypto payment processors (for crypto orders)

We do **not** store your card, bank, or wallet details. Each provider handles payment data securely under its own privacy and security policies.

We may retain **basic transaction details** (payment status, transaction ID, amount, currency) **only** to:
- Verify your purchase
- Resolve support tickets
- Confirm ownership of an order

We also use other service providers, including:
- **Discord, Google, and GitHub** — for account sign-in and support. We store the user ID (and, where provided, name, email and image) that the provider returns at sign-in.
- **Hosting and infrastructure providers** (including server hosting and CDN) — which may process standard technical data such as server logs.

Any other provider we use from time to time is covered by its own privacy policy. We share **only the minimum data** each provider needs to perform its function, and each provider's handling of that data is governed by **its own privacy policy**.

## 4. UK GDPR & EU GDPR

Where you are in the **United Kingdom**, this policy complies with the **UK GDPR** and the **Data Protection Act 2018**. Where you are in the **European Union**, it complies with the **EU GDPR**.

We process your personal data on the following legal bases:
- **Contract** — to provide the services you purchase
- **Legitimate interests** — security, fraud prevention, order recordkeeping, and support
- **Consent** — where a specific feature requires it

You have the right to:
- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correction** of inaccurate data
- **Erasure** of your data (subject to records we are required to keep)
- **Objection** to processing
- **Data portability**
- **Complaint** to the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)** in the UK, or to your local supervisory authority in the EU

To exercise any of these rights, contact us through the channel in section 11.
